ℹ️ About the Project

This dashboard is a prototype digital surveillance system that tracks public health discussions on social media to detect early signals of disease outbreaks.

Methodology

  • Data Collection: Tweets and search trends related to symptoms and diseases are gathered (simulated in this version).
  • Data Processing: Removes noise, standardizes language, and maps mentions to locations.
  • Trend Analysis: Visualizes frequency over time using charts.
  • Sentiment Analysis: Determines emotional tone of posts.
  • Prediction: Identifies spikes that may signal outbreaks.
