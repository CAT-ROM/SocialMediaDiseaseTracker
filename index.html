Infectious Disease Monitoring Dashboard

Integrating Social Media Insights & Public Health Data

Methodology Overview

  1. Data Collection: Social media platforms, Google Trends, health reports, crowdsourced surveys.
  2. Data Processing: Noise removal, language standardization, geolocation mapping.
  3. Identifying Patterns & Trends: Symptom trends, sentiment analysis, epidemiological correlation.
  4. Model Development & Prediction: Machine learning models, NLP, anomaly detection.
  5. Validation & Integration: Cross-check with official data, refine models, interactive visualization.
